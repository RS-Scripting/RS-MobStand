# Creating Your First Mob Stand

This guide will walk you through converting an ordinary Armor Stand into a fully functional Mob Stand.

---

# Requirements

Before creating a Mob Stand, ensure you have:

* An Armor Stand
* A Stick
* Permission to use RS-MobStand

---

# Step 1: Place an Armor Stand

Place an Armor Stand where you want your Mob Stand to operate.

Choose a location that is close to the mobs you want to manage.

---

# Step 2: Convert the Armor Stand

Hold a **Stick** in your hand.

Sneak and right-click the Armor Stand.

The Armor Stand will immediately convert into a Mob Stand.

Once converted:

* The Armor Stand becomes a Mob Stand.
* Ownership is assigned to the player who performed the conversion.
* The Mob Stand is ready to be configured.

---

# Step 3: Open the Mob Stand Menu

Right-click your Mob Stand to open the main configuration menu.

From this menu you can configure:

* Scan Radius
* Leave Alive
* Adult Filters
* Baby Filters
* Gravity
* Pause / Resume

---

# Ownership

Mob Stands are owned by the player who creates them.

By default:

* Owners can modify their own Mob Stands.
* Other players cannot change settings.
* Administrators may bypass ownership restrictions.

---

# Converting Back to an Armor Stand

If you no longer need a Mob Stand:

1. Open the Mob Stand menu.
2. Select **Convert Back to Armor Stand**.

The Mob Stand will immediately return to a normal Armor Stand.

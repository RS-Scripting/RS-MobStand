# Pause and Resume

## Overview

A Mob Stand can be **paused** when you want to temporarily stop it from managing mobs.
Pausing a Mob Stand does not remove its configuration.

---

## Pausing a Mob Stand
To pause a Mob Stand:

1. Open the Mob Stand menu.
2. Select **Pause**.
3. The Mob Stand stops managing mobs.

The Mob Stand remains in place with its existing settings.

---

## Resuming a Mob Stand
To resume a paused Mob Stand:

1. Open the Mob Stand menu.
2. Select **Resume**.
3. The Mob Stand begins managing mobs again using its current settings.

---

## Mob Stands Start Paused
When a standard Armor Stand is converted into an RS-MobStand, the new Mob Stand starts **paused**.
This gives you an opportunity to configure the Mob Stand before it begins managing mobs.

A typical setup is:

1. Convert the Armor Stand.
2. Open the Mob Stand menu.
3. Configure the mob filters.
4. Set the scan radius.
5. Configure any other options.
6. Select **Resume** when you are ready.

---

## When to Pause a Mob Stand
Pausing can be useful when:

- You are changing the Mob Stand's configuration.
- You are working in the area around the Mob Stand.
- You temporarily do not want the Mob Stand managing mobs.
- You are performing maintenance on a mob farm.

---

## Configuration Is Preserved
Pausing a Mob Stand does not require you to configure it again when you resume it.
Its configured filters, radius, and other settings remain available.

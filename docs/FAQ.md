# Frequently Asked Questions

---

## How do I create a Mob Stand?

Place an Armor Stand.

Sneak and right-click it while holding a Stick.

The Armor Stand will immediately convert into a Mob Stand.

---

## Can other players modify my Mob Stand?

No.

Only the owner can modify a Mob Stand unless a player has administrative permissions.

---

## How do I remove a Mob Stand?

Open the Main Menu and select:

```text id="y2jlwm"
Convert Back to Armor Stand
```

The Mob Stand will immediately return to a normal Armor Stand.

---

## Do Mob Stands survive server restarts?

Yes.

All Mob Stand settings are saved automatically.

---

## Can I have multiple Mob Stands?

Yes.

Players may create as many Mob Stands as allowed by server rules and performance considerations.

---

## Why is my Mob Stand not processing mobs?

Check:

* Pause Status
* Radius Settings
* Adult Filters
* Baby Filters
* Leave Alive Settings

See the Troubleshooting guide for detailed assistance.

---

## What does Leave Alive do?

Leave Alive determines how many matching mobs remain alive.

Example:

```text id="0g7vqf"
Leave Alive: 5
```

Five matching mobs will always remain.

---

## What does Gravity do?

Gravity determines whether the Mob Stand behaves like a normal Armor Stand.

Enabled:

```text id="pnnc4e"
Falls normally
```

Disabled:

```text id="70x2p4"
Remains fixed in place
```

---

## Can administrators bypass ownership?

Yes.

Players with:

```text id="x2tq67"
rsmobstand.admin
```

may bypass ownership restrictions.

---

## Does RS-MobStand require any dependencies?

No.

RS-MobStand operates independently and does not require additional plugins.

---
